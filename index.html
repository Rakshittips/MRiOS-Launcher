Is MRiOS Launcher safe to install? +
Yes — the APK is scanned before release and doesn't request any permissions beyond what a standard launcher needs.
Will it drain my battery? +
The MRiOS animations are GPU-accelerated and lightweight. You can also disable the live wallpaper engine in Settings to save power.
Can I keep my existing icon pack? +
Yes, third-party icon packs are supported, though the morph animation looks best with the built-in MRiOS icon set.
